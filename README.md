# Project A.T.L.A.S. (English Version)

# Project A.T.L.A.S. #

- **Publico Alvo:**
> The target audience for the game consists of people aged 12 and over who are interested in digital games, especially in the incremental genre, and who appreciate mechanics related to exploration, discovery, and progression.
- **Tipo de Jogo:** 
> Single-player, 2D pixel art.
- **Target Platforms:** 
> Project A.T.L.A.S. will be developed for PC (Windows), with Steam and itch.io as distribution and commercialization platforms.
- **Genre:** 
> The game blends two game genres: Shoot 'Em Up and Incremental.
- **Number of Players:** 
> Only one player is supported; the game is single-player.
- **Projected Release Date:** 
> August 2027

## High Concept Statement ##
> In a dystopian ocean devastated by accumulated trash, the player takes control of Naut, an A.T.L.A.S. submariner, with the mission of exploring contaminated regions, facing Animalixos — animals affected by pollution — and restoring the marine environment. Through combat, exploration, and submarine upgrades, the player advances through increasingly deep areas, facing challenges and Guardians responsible for the contamination of each region.
> 
> The game combines elements of Shoot 'Em Up and incremental progression with a setting focused on environmental restoration. While facing the dangers of the ocean, the player evolves their submarine and visually tracks the transformation of a degraded environment into a more vibrant ecosystem. The proposal aims to evoke a sense of discovery, satisfaction, and relaxation, differentiating itself by using the recovery of a polluted ocean not just as a narrative theme, but as part of the player's own progression and experience.

## Feature Set ##
> - **Submarine exploration:** Exploring a dystopian ocean divided into contaminated regions.
> - **Shoot 'Em Up combat:** Facing Animalixos using the submarine.
> - **Environmental recovery:** Purifying contaminated areas as the central objective of progression.
> - **Incremental progression:** Obtaining Bio-Credits to upgrade the submarine.
> - **Zone Guardians:** Encounters that represent the main challenge of each region and its recovery.
> - **Visual environmental transformation:** Changing a degraded, desaturated ocean into a more vibrant environment as recovery progresses.
> - **Lixopedia:** A reference system with information on waste and Animalixos encountered during exploration.
> - **Setting and environmental narrative:** Using marine pollution as an element integrated into the world, narrative, and game experience.
> - **Exploration and discovery experience:** Combining progression, discovery, and atmosphere to provide a satisfying and contemplative experience.

## Team Roles ##
> - **Daniel Ruiz Rodriguez:** Game Designer, primarily responsible for drafting and organizing the GDD, developing the core idea of the project, organizing design activities, and balancing mechanics.
> - **James Correa Silva:** Lead Programmer, responsible for implementing game systems, team organization, and managing deadlines and tasks.
> - **Leonardo Isys de Araujo Santos:** Writer and responsible for narrative construction, developing narrative elements, and conducting project-related research.
> - **Pierre Santos Sousa:** 2D Artist and responsible for the direction and production of the project's visual and audio elements.

## The Competition ##
> The main competitors and references for Project A.T.L.A.S. are *Shelldiver*, *Dave the Diver*, and *Subnautica*, as they present different elements related to marine environment exploration, progression, and interaction with the ocean.
> 
> *Shelldiver* is closest to the project by combining submarine exploration, pixel art aesthetics, and action elements. *Dave the Diver* uses ocean exploration as a core part of its experience, combining different genres and progression systems. Meanwhile, *Subnautica* stands out for exploration and survival in a submarine environment, with a strong focus on world discovery and interaction with its elements.
> 
> Project A.T.L.A.S. seeks to differentiate itself by combining exploration and submarine combat with a proposal centered on environmental recovery. Instead of using the ocean merely as a setting or exploration space, the game transforms pollution and environmental recovery into central elements of progression, using the visual transformation of the ocean and interaction with Animalixos to reinforce this proposal.

## Innovation/Creativity ##
> The main creative differential of Project A.T.L.A.S. is integrating environmental education into the game experience, using exploration and mechanics to present the problem of ocean pollution interactively. The theme is not only presented through text, but also represented by the environment, the Animalixos, and the visual recovery of the ocean, making learning a natural part of the player's experience.

## Scope Management ##
> To ensure project completion within available resources, the scope will be divided according to the priority level of features:
> 
> **Green Light — Priority**
> - Shoot laser/projectile.
> - Player and enemy health system.
> - Bio-Credits and Oxygen collection.
> - Oxygen as run time limit.
> - Navigation speed.
> - Submarine upgrades.
> - Inanimate trash as contact enemies.
> - Animalixos with unique abilities.
> - English language.
> - Settings/pause menu.
> - Upgrade screen.
> 
> **Yellow Light — Conditional**
> - Secret upgrades unlocked by eliminating specific enemies.
> - Complete area cleanup after defeating the boss.
> - Depth zones as stages.
> - Ocean pressure mechanic.
> - One boss per zone.
> - Volume control.
> - Exit/close game button.
> - Lixopedia.
> - Buttons/shortcuts screen.
> 
> **Red Light — Optional**
> - Resolution options.
> - Custom shortcut configuration.
> - Other non-essential features and menus.

# AVISOS E CONTROLES DE DESENVOLVIMENTO

## PROJETO A.T.L.A.S.
Projeto A.T.L.A.S. é um shoot ’em up incremental ambientado em um futuro onde o lixo tomou conta dos oceanos e corrompeu as antigas Guardiãs, criaturas responsáveis por proteger e purificar as águas. Como agente da A.T.L.A.S., você explora mares infestados de Animalixos — criaturas marinhas fundidas com lixo — limpando-as e reciclando os resíduos para obter recursos. Use seus ganhos para melhorar o submarino, alcançar novas áreas, salvar as Guardiãs e reconstruir o ecossistema dos oceanos.

## AVISOS
Esta versão do jogo se encontra em versão alpha, não contendo muito de suas mecânicas principais, polimento e podendo apresentar bugs.

## ORIENTAÇÕES PARA INSTALAÇÃO
Passo a passo para playtest (SOMENTE WINDOWS):
- Descompacte o arquivo "Projeto ATLAS 0.1.zip";
- Navegue até encontrar "PROJETO A.T.L.A.S.exe";
- Clique duas vezes sobre o executavel;
- Ignore as mensagens de erro e inicie o game.

## CONTROLES
- W A S D - Movimentação;
- Click Esquerdo do mouse - Atirar projéteis;
- R - Voltar ou reiniciar o jogo.

## GAMEPLAY
Seu objetivo é navegar no oceano, desviando das rochas marinhas, atirando projeteis nos animalixos e coletando os Eco-créditos antes que o timer zere!

## GUI
- Barra de vida;
- Eco-créditos coletados;
- Oxigenio sobrando.
